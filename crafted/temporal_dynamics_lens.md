# Lens: Temporal Dynamics
Type: Lens

A lens that tracks change velocity and acceleration over time.

## Application
Apply to trends, decisions, patterns to understand not just WHERE going, but HOW FAST.

## The Questions
1. Velocity: How fast is this changing per unit time?
2. Acceleration: Is the rate of change increasing or decreasing?
3. Inflection Points: Where does acceleration reverse sign?
4. Momentum: How much time until velocity stabilizes?

## Example
Before: "Company X is growing."
After: "Growing at 15%%/year, accelerating from 5%%/year, hits saturation in 3 years."

## Quality Indicator
If temporal analysis does not change urgency, velocity not measured precisely enough.
