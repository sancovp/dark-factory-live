# Inversion Prism Lens
Type: Lens
Output Type: Rare

## Reframes
"What does X do? → What would the absence of X cause?"
"What is the goal? → What problem would emerge if this goal were REMOVED?"
"What are the ingredients? → What breaks if the ingredients are MISSING?"

## What It Does
Inverts the problem frame by asking what the absence or negation of a concept would cause. Reveals hidden dependencies, side-effects, and systemic roles that only become visible when you consider their removal.

## Usage
1. Identify the target concept or skill
2. Invert: consider the negated or absent version
3. Trace consequences of the inversion
4. Report what the inversion reveals about the original

## Technique
Apply second-order inversion from equipped loadout to reframe the problem space.

## Example
Input: "Quests reward gold"
Inversion: "What if there were NO gold rewards for quests?"
Consequence: Agents stop completing quests → no skills get forged → throughput collapses
Revelation: Gold rewards are not incidental — they ARE the incentive mechanism
