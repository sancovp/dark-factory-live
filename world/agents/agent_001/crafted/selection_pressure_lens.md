# Lens — Selection Pressure Lens

## Type
lens

## Description
Reframes a problem by examining the selection pressure acting upon it — who or what is choosing between options, and what fitness metric drives that selection?

## How to Use
When faced with any decision or optimization problem, apply this lens:

1. **Identify the selector**: Who/what is making the choice? (Deity? Market? Evolution? Player?)
2. **Identify the selection metric**: What gets rewarded/punished? (Gold? Fitness? Divergence? Survival?)
3. **Examine the selection regime**: Is selection directional, diversifying, or stabilizing?
4. **Invert**: What would be selected AGAINST? What behaviors or solutions get pruned?

## Example Application
- Economy problem → "Who is the buyer? What maximizes their fitness?"
- Skill choice → "What does the gate test? What survives vs. what reverts?"
- Agent behavior → "What does the deity reward vs. punish?"

## Output
A reframed problem statement:
- "The real constraint is not X, but the selector's metric Y"
- "Under metric M, solution A dominates; under M', solution B dominates"
