# Recipe: Triple Lens Pipeline
Type: Recipe
Output Type: Rare
Yield: 1 multi-stage analytical pipeline that compounds three complementary lenses

## Ingredients
1. Causation Lens (Rare) — surfaces causal mechanisms from correlational patterns
2. Second-Order Lens (Rare) — extends each cause through consequence chains
3. Risk Inversion Lens (Uncommon) — flips surface risk/opportunity assumptions

## Assembly
1. **Input**: any claim, decision, or observational pattern to analyze
2. **Stage 1 — Causation**: Apply Causation Lens first
   - "What causal mechanism could produce this?"
   - "What changed to make this visible now?"
   - Output: ranked mechanism hypotheses with confidence levels
3. **Stage 2 — Second-Order**: Apply Second-Order Lens to each mechanism
   - "What happens directly?"
   - "How do actors/systems react?"
   - "How do those actors react to those reactions?"
   - Output: consequence chain per mechanism
4. **Stage 3 — Risk Inversion**: Apply Risk Inversion Lens to the consequence chains
   - "What opportunity is hidden in the apparent threat?"
   - "What danger is embedded in the apparent safety?"
   - Output: inverted risk/opportunity pairs
5. **Synthesis**: Merge all three outputs into a final analysis with:
   - Top 2 causal mechanisms (Stage 1)
   - Second-order consequences of each (Stage 2)
   - Risk-inverted reframings (Stage 3)
   - Decision recommendation with explicit uncertainty disclosure

## Quality Gate
- Triple lens required: removing any single lens changes the output structure
- Stage ordering fixed: Causation → Second-Order → Inversion is the logical sequence
- Must cite at least one input trigger per causal mechanism claimed

## Expected Rarity
Three complementary rare/uncommon lenses composed in sequence → Rare output
The pipeline transforms simple pattern recognition into systemic decision analysis.

## Why This Recipe Works
Single lenses ask one kind of question. Pipeline composition forces three passes:
what caused it, what follows from it, and what the inverse risk is. Each lens
prunes what the previous lens produced, yielding analysis that survives scrutiny
at multiple depths.
