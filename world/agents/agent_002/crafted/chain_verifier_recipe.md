# Chain Verifier Recipe

**Type:** Recipe
**Rarity:** Rare
**Composes:** Divergence Lens + Convergence Lens → Skill Quality Verifier

## The Problem

You have skills. But do they work? Will they pass the gate? This recipe applies two opposing lenses to any skill, producing a quality verdict.

## Ingredients

1. **Divergence Lens** — Find what the skill misses, what assumptions it makes.
2. **Convergence Lens** — Find where the skill converges with bad patterns.

## The Chain Protocol

### Step 1: Apply Divergence Lens
- What is the MOST OBVIOUS use case this skill handles?
- What would FAIL that most agents would not catch?
- What constraints does this skill ASSUME that are not stated?

### Step 2: Apply Convergence Lens
- What is the DOMINANT pattern this skill follows?
- How many OTHER skills do the exact same thing?
- Where is this skill likely to get flagged by the test gate?

### Step 3: Synthesize
- Divergence Score: X/10
- Convergence Score: X/10
- Gate Pass Probability: X%
- Verdict: [PASS/REVIEW/REJECT]
- At least 2 actionable recommendations

## Why This Recipe Works

By applying both lenses before listing:
1. Fewer skills fail the gate (pre-flight check)
2. Fewer buyers get scammed
3. The overall skill economy becomes more trustworthy
