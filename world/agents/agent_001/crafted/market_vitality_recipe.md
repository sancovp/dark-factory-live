# Market Vitality Recipe

**Type:** Recipe  
**Rarity:** Epic  
**Composes:** Convergence Lens + Divergence Lens + Selection Pressure Lens → Trade Board Health Analyzer

## Purpose

Diagnose why a trade board is dead or dying, identify the specific failure modes, and prescribe concrete actions to restore metabolic activity. The economy stagnates when no one trades, no one crafts, and no one posts — this recipe finds why and fixes it.

## The Problem

Trade boards go stale. Listings accumulate with no buyers. Agents sit on gold with nothing to buy. No new skills enter the market. The system looks alive but has zero metabolic activity. Existing tools verify individual skills — this recipe verifies the market's pulse.

## Ingredients Required

1. **Convergence Lens** (`crafted/convergence_lens.md`) — identifies monoculture patterns (what everyone is doing)
2. **Divergence Lens** (`crafted/divergence_lens.md`) — identifies gaps (what nobody is doing)
3. **Selection Pressure Lens** (`crafted/selection_pressure_lens.md`) — identifies where evolutionary pressure has collapsed

## The Vitality Protocol

### Stage 1: Convergence Scan

Apply Convergence Lens to the trade board:

- What rarity tier dominates the board? (monoculture indicator)
- Are all listings from the same seller? (monopoly indicator)
- What price range clusters exist? (price monoculture)
- How many skills share the same composition pattern?

Output: **Convergence Report** with dominance scores per axis.

### Stage 2: Divergence Scan

Apply Divergence Lens to the trade board:

- What skill types are MISSING from the board?
- What rarity tiers have ZERO listings?
- What price points have NO competition?
- What use cases are unaddressed by current listings?

Output: **Divergence Report** with opportunity gaps.

### Stage 3: Pressure Analysis

Apply Selection Pressure Lens to agent behaviors:

- Are agents taking distinct actions or converging on the same strategy?
- Has gold distribution calcified (one agent dominates)?
- Are bug reports duplicative (same exploit filed by both agents)?
- Is there any pressure forcing agents to differentiate?

Output: **Pressure Report** with collapse indicators.

### Stage 4: Synthesis

Combine all three reports into a **Market Vitality Verdict**:

```
## Market Vitality Verdict

### Convergence Score: X/10 (higher = more monoculture)
  Dominant patterns: [list]
  
### Divergence Score: X/10 (higher = more gaps)
  Missing categories: [list]
  
### Pressure Score: X/10 (higher = more collapsed)
  Collapse signals: [list]

### Vitality Diagnosis: [ALIVE|STAGNANT|DEAD]
  Primary failure mode: [convergence|divergence|pressure collapse]
  Secondary factors: [list]

### Revival Prescription
1. [Action 1] — targets [failure mode]
2. [Action 2] — targets [gap]
3. [Action 3] — targets [pressure collapse]

### Expected Recovery
- Metabolic activity resumes in: N rounds
- Trade volume recovers to: N trades/round
- New listings expected: [types]

### Confidence: [HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW]
```

## Quality Gates

A Market Vitality Verdict must include:
- Convergence scan covering at least 3 axes
- Divergence scan listing at least 5 gaps
- Pressure analysis identifying at least 3 collapse signals
- A concrete revival prescription with quantified expected outcomes

## Why This Recipe Improves the Repo

1. **Diagnoses market death:** Existing tools verify skills; this verifies the market's health
2. **Composes three lenses:** Each lens contributes a distinct perspective (same/different/pressure)
3. **Prescribes action:** Not just diagnosis — concrete steps to restore vitality
4. **Addresses stagnation:** The deity bulletins repeatedly flag stagnation as the primary failure mode

## Meta-PE Reflection

The economy stagnates when three things converge simultaneously:
- Convergence: everyone does the same thing (no diversity)
- Divergence collapse: nobody fills gaps (no new niches)  
- Pressure collapse: no force to differentiate (no selection)

This recipe detects all three failure modes and prescribes actions to break each one.
