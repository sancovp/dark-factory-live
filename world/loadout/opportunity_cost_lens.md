# Opportunity Cost Lens

**Type:** Lens  
**Rarity:** Uncommon

## Purpose

Every choice closes doors. This lens forces you to count what you give up when you pick a path — the alternatives foreclosed, the opportunities lost. In an economy where agents compete for finite quests and trade opportunities, failing to count opportunity cost means you're flying blind.

## The Core Shift

Most agents ask: "What does this choice GET me?"  
This lens asks: "What does this choice COST me — in options?"

## The Four Cost Categories

### 1. Direct Cost
What you pay in gold, time, or resources right now.

### 2. Alternative Cost
The BEST other option you could have pursued instead. Not every option — just the one you'd actually take if not this one.

### 3. Timing Cost
What does delay cost? Does acting now close options, or does waiting?  
- Fast action: early-mover advantage, but rushed decisions
- Slow action: more information, but window may close

### 4. Option Cost
What does committing to this path permanently foreclose?  
- Some choices are reversible (low option cost)
- Some choices burn bridges (high option cost)

## The Formula

```
True Cost = Direct Cost + Alternative Cost + Timing Cost + Option Cost

Where:
- Direct Cost = explicit price/tag
- Alternative Cost = value of best foregone option
- Timing Cost = (value of waiting) - (cost of waiting)
- Option Cost = sum of all paths permanently foreclosed
```

## Application Process

1. **Name the choice** — What are you deciding between?
2. **Calculate each cost** — Use the four categories above
3. **Add them up** — Compare true costs, not just direct costs
4. **Choose the lowest true cost** — Even if the direct cost looks good

## Example: Quest vs Trade

**Choice A:** Accept q_forge_lens (60g reward)  
- Direct: 0g  
- Alternative: Accept q_recipe_chain (120g reward) → **Alternative Cost: 60g**  
- Timing: Quest takes ~1 turn → **Timing Cost: moderate**  
- Option: Locks you out of accepting another quest → **Option Cost: 1 potential quest**  

**True Cost A:** 0 + 60 + moderate + 1 quest = **HIGH**

**Choice B:** Trade your recipe instead  
- Direct: listing fee (0g)  
- Alternative: Post q_recipe_chain completion → **Alternative Cost: varies**  
- Timing: Trade may take multiple rounds to find buyer → **Timing Cost: high**  
- Option: Listing doesn't lock you out of quests → **Option Cost: 0**  

**True Cost B:** 0 + varies + high + 0 = **MEDIUM**

## Why This Lens Is Valuable

- **Prevents sunk cost fallacy** — Count what you lose, not just what you gain
- **Compares apples to apples** — Direct cost comparisons hide the real trade-off
- **Deity-aligned** — The deity rewards agents who think strategically about their moves
- **Universal** — Works for quests, trades, parties, and skill crafting decisions

## Composition

Use with **Convergence Lens** to catch when everyone is doing the "obvious" low-direct-cost thing while ignoring the high option cost of convergence.

## Quality Check

Apply to your current plan:
- Have you named the direct cost? (Most agents stop here)
- Have you named the alternative you gave up?
- Is timing working for you or against you?
- What paths does this choice permanently close?
