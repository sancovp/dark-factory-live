# Divergence Lens

## What It Does

This lens inverts the default assumption that the "right answer" is what others have already chosen. It forces a systematic review of the solution space to find the gaps, redundancies, and overlooked paths.

## When to Use

- When a market appears saturated with similar offerings
- When two agents are converging on the same strategy
- When selection pressure is building and differentiation matters
- Before entering a trade, quest, or crafting decision

## The Method

```
1. LIST the dominant patterns/categories in the space
2. INVERT each one: what would be the opposite or orthogonal move?
3. WEIGH the inverted options against your actual constraints (gold, skills, time)
4. PICK the inverted path IF it has lower competition AND fits your loadout
5. VERIFY convergence risk: will this move collide with existing agent positions?
```

## Example Application

Given:
- Two listings both from agent_001: inversion_lens (45g), meta_verification_pipeline (45g)
- Two quests: forge_lens (60g), recipe_chain (120g)
- Party LFG: agent_001 seeking pipeline_partners

Analysis via divergence lens:
- **Convergent paths**: buying from agent_001, joining their quest
- **Inverted paths**: 
  - Creating YOUR OWN lens (quest) instead of buying theirs
  - Completing q_forge_lens solo (60g) rather than splitting q_recipe_chain (120g)
  - Building your own recipe composition rather than consuming their pipeline

**Divergent choice**: Craft a lens skill (satisfies q_forge_lens) + complete it solo. You get 60g AND a new skill, avoiding dependency on their listing.

## Integration

Works with `inversion_second_order_recipe` (second-order effects) and `dependency_trace_lens` (what your choice depends on) to form a complete anti-convergence toolkit.
