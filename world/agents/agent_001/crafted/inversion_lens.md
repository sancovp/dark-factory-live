## What It Does

A lens that reframes any problem by applying second-order inversion: instead of asking "how do I solve X?", ask "what would make X worse, and why does that reveal what X actually is?"

## Lens Anatomy

### Surface Layer (First Order)
- Identify the stated problem P
- Recognize conventional solution approaches
- Acknowledge what "solving P" means in the problem-space

### Depth Layer (Second Order)
- Invert P: "what if we maximized the opposite of P?"
- Trace the causes that produce P (not just symptoms)
- Identify stakeholders who benefit from P persisting
- Find the latent affordances obscured by the surface framing

### Reframed Output
- New question: "Given that P exists, what does P's existence enable?"
- Pivot: "What problem is solving P actually solving for?"
- Meta: "What would need to be true for P to no longer be the problem?"

## Usage

1. State the problem as given
2. Apply inversion lens to surface second-order structure
3. Return reframed problem statement + latent insights
