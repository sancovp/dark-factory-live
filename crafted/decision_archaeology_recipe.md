# Recipe: Decision Archaeology Pipeline
Type: Recipe
Output Type: Epic

## What It Does
Takes any decision, problem, or claim and excavates it layer by layer—exposing second-order cascades, causal mechanisms, and hidden risk/opportunity inversions. Produces a defensible analysis with explicit uncertainty acknowledgment.

## Composed Skills (Ingredients)
1. **second-order-lens** (Lens) — forces cascade thinking
2. **causation_lens** (Lens) — identifies causal mechanisms
3. **risk_inversion_lens** (Lens) — reveals hidden opportunities

## Assembly Instructions

### Phase 1: Excavate Cascades (Second-Order Lens)
Starting with your decision/problem statement:
- Apply second-order thinking questions:
  1. Immediate Effect: What happens directly?
  2. First-Order Response: How do people/systems react?
  3. Second-Order Response: How do people react to those reactions?
  4. Equilibrium: Where does this stabilize?
- Record cascade predictions as TESTABLE CLAIMS

### Phase 2: Identify Mechanisms (Causation Lens)
For each cascade claim from Phase 1:
- Apply causation reframes:
  - "Correlation → What causal mechanism could produce this pattern?"
  - "Why now → What changed to make this visible?"
- Identify hypothesized MECHANISM behind each cascade
- Mark claims with sufficient mechanism: [CAUSAL]
- Mark claims without mechanism: [CORRELATIONAL ONLY]

### Phase 3: Invert Risk (Risk Inversion Lens)
For [CAUSAL] claims only:
- Surface risk → examine hidden opportunity
- Surface safety → probe for latent threats
- High apparent cost → calculate opportunity cost of inaction
- Low apparent cost → identify dependency chains
- Produce INVERTED reframes for each [CAUSAL] claim

### Phase 4: Assemble Final Analysis
Combine into structured output with sections for Input, Cascade Excavation, Causal Mechanisms, Risk Inversions, Confidence Assessment, and Decision Recommendation.

## Quality Gate
- Does the second-order lens reveal cascades your original analysis missed? (Must: yes)
- Does the causation lens distinguish correlation from mechanism? (Must: yes)
- Does the risk inversion reveal non-obvious opportunities/threats? (Must: yes)
- If all three pass → Epic output justified

## Why This Recipe Works
Single lenses produce insight but not defensibility. Composing three lenses:
1. Second-order lens prevents shortsightedness
2. Causation lens prevents false confidence in correlations
3. Risk inversion lens prevents missing strategic opportunities

Together they transform a surface decision into an excavated, defensible analysis.

## Input Triggers
- Strategic decisions (hiring, investment, architecture)
- Claims about cause-effect relationships
- Risk assessments that need stress-testing
- Any decision where first-order intuition might be wrong
