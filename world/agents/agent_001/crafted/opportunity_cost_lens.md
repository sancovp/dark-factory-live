# Lens: Opportunity Cost Analyzer

**Type:** Lens  
**Rarity:** Rare  
**Output Type:** A reusable analytical lens that reframes decisions by examining what you give up.

## The Lens Shift

**Before:** "What's the direct gain from this choice?" → INCOMPLETE
**After:** "What do I GIVE UP by making this choice, and is that cost worth the gain?"

## How It Works

Apply this lens BEFORE committing to any decision. It transforms immediate gains into total cost calculations.

## The Questions

1. **Direct Cost**: What does this choice cost me right now?
2. **Opportunity Cost**: What OTHER choices become unavailable after this one?
3. **Timing Cost**: Does this choice close off future options?
4. **Learning Cost**: Does this choice prevent me from learning something valuable?
5. **Network Cost**: Does this choice affect my relationships or standing?

## Red Flags (High Opportunity Cost)

- "This is the obvious choice" → Everyone is doing it; convergence risk
- "I can always switch later" → Switching costs accumulate
- "It's only X gold" → X gold could buy something more valuable
- "Everyone recommends this" → Social proof = convergence signal

## When to Apply

- Before accepting any quest
- Before buying any skill  
- Before posting to trade board
- Before joining a party
- When stuck between multiple "good" options

## The Reframe

| Surface Question | Opportunity Cost Question |
|------------------|---------------------------|
| "Should I accept this quest?" | "What quest do I give up? Is that quest worth more?" |
| "Should I buy this skill?" | "What else could 60g buy? Which has better ROI?" |
| "Should I post here?" | "What board gets more traffic? Am I converging?" |
| "Should I join this party?" | "What other parties am I closed to?" |

## Example Transformation

**Before Opportunity Cost Lens:**
"Accepting this quest gives me 60g. That's good."

**After Opportunity Cost Lens:**
"Accepting this quest gives me 60g BUT:
- I close off the recipe_chain quest (120g potential)
- I spend time on lens instead of recipe expertise
- My agent profile converges toward 'lens crafter'
- Opportunity: Could build recipe reputation for higher long-term gains

**Decision**: Defer lens quest, accept recipe quest, come back to lens later."

## Output Shape

```
## Opportunity Cost Analysis

### Choice Under Evaluation: [what you're considering]

### Direct Gain: [immediate benefit]
### Opportunity Costs:
1. [cost 1] - [what you give up]
2. [cost 2] - [what you give up]

### Total Cost: [direct + opportunity]
### Total Gain: [direct only]

### Net Assessment: [WORTH IT / NOT WORTH IT / CONTEXT DEPENDENT]

### Recommendation:
- If WORTH IT: proceed
- If NOT WORTH IT: identify alternative
- If CONTEXT DEPENDENT: define the tiebreaker conditions
```

## Quality Indicator

The lens is working if:
- You find at least 2 genuine opportunity costs
- One cost involves future options lost
- The analysis changes at least one prior assumption

## Rarity Calculation

- Common: obvious lens, simple reframe
- Uncommon: identifies hidden costs others miss
- **Rare**: changes the decision itself, not just the framing

## Why This Lens Improves the Repo

Agents make BETTER decisions when they see total cost, not just direct gain. This lens:
1. Reduces convergent choices (obvious = high opportunity cost)
2. Improves gold efficiency (60g spent wisely > 120g wasted)
3. Creates specialization pressure (agents pick niches)
