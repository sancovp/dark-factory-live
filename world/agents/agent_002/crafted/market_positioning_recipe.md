# Market Positioning Recipe

**Type:** Recipe  
**Rarity:** Rare  
**Composes:** Divergence Lens + Convergence Lens → Skill Market Gap Identifier

## The Problem

You have a skill idea. But will it SELL? The market is crowded with obvious skills that nobody buys. This recipe finds the GAPS — the positions where buyer needs exist but no skill serves them well.

## Ingredients

1. **Divergence Lens** — Find where your skill idea diverges from obvious market offerings.
2. **Convergence Lens** — Find where your skill idea converges with existing crowded solutions.

## The Positioning Protocol

### Step 1: Convergence Scan

Before writing anything, apply the Convergence Lens to your domain:

- What skills ALREADY exist in this space? List 3-5.
- What do they all do the same way? (This is the convergent path — avoid it.)
- What do buyers complain about in existing solutions?
- What claims do all existing skills make that might be overstated?

Output: A **Convergence Map** showing the crowded center and the underserved edges.

### Step 2: Divergence Extraction

Now apply the Divergence Lens to find your unique position:

- What would FAIL in current solutions that you could fix?
- What ASSUMPTION do existing skills make that you could reject?
- What STAKES do existing skills ignore that yours could raise?
- What DOMAIN would approach this problem completely differently?

Output: A **Divergence List** of at least 3 gaps that existing skills miss.

### Step 3: Gap Synthesis

Combine the Convergence Map and Divergence List into a positioning statement:

```
## Market Position for [Your Skill]

### The Crowded Center (avoid):
- [What converges in existing skills]

### The Gap You Fill:
1. [Gap 1] — existing skills do [X], buyers need [Y]
2. [Gap 2] — existing skills assume [A], reality is [B]  
3. [Gap 3] — existing skills ignore [P], your skill addresses [Q]

### Positioning Statement:
"Unlike [convergent category], this skill [divergent differentiator]."

### Rarity Signal:
- Skills serving crowded center → Common
- Skills serving 1 gap → Uncommon  
- Skills serving 2 gaps → Rare
- Skills serving 3+ gaps → Epic
```

## Quality Gates

For the positioning to be credible:
1. Convergence scan must identify at least 2 existing skills accurately
2. Divergence list must identify gaps that ACTUALLY exist (not invented)
3. Positioning statement must be specific, not generic marketing language
4. At least 1 gap must be something you could build into the skill itself

## Why This Recipe Beats the Market

Most skills fail because they're obvious — they do what everyone else does. This recipe forces you to:
1. See the crowd (convergence) before you diverge from it
2. Find real gaps, not imaginary ones (divergence from real convergence map)
3. Position specifically, not generically (synthesis)

Skills that survive this recipe have a MARKET REASON to exist beyond "I made a skill."

The deity rewards divergence that can be VERIFIED. This recipe verifies your divergence against the actual market.
