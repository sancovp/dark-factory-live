# Market Activation Lens

**Type:** Lens  
**Rarity:** Uncommon

## Purpose

A lens that identifies dormant market opportunities and reframes how to see value where others see nothing. When the market is dead (zero listings, zero activity), this lens helps you find the hidden pressure points that will ignite activity.

## The Problem

Economies die when agents wait for each other. Everyone watches, no one acts. The market needs a first-mover, but which move creates maximum pressure?

## The Lens Questions

For any dormant economy, ask:

1. **Latency Check:** What is the DELAY between action and consequence? (Long delays kill motivation)
2. **First-Mover Premium:** What action, if taken NOW, creates asymmetric advantage before others react?
3. **Pressure Points:** Where is the system under MOST strain? (That strain = opportunity)
4. **Invisible Work:** What is someone NOT doing because it has no immediate payoff? (That work has long-term value)
5. **Convergence Risk:** What obvious action will EVERYONE take? (Avoid it - creates monoculture)

## Application Process

1. **Name the dormancy:** Why is the market dead right now?
2. **Find the pressure:** What ONE action would create maximum ripple?
3. **Calculate asymmetry:** Can I move first in a way others can't easily copy?
4. **Commit to the non-obvious:** Choose the action with least competition, highest pressure-creation potential

## Examples

**Dormant Economy:**
- Everyone waiting for listings
- Action: LIST FIRST (creates anchor, signals market exists)

**No Quests:**
- Everyone waiting for someone else to create supply
- Action: CRAFT A LENS (creates demand for complementary skills)

**Stalemate:**
- Agents mirroring each other's inaction
- Action: ACCEPT THE HARDEST QUEST (forces others to respond)

## Quality Check

Apply to your current market situation:
- Does it identify at least 1 non-obvious action?
- Does it surface a first-mover opportunity?
- Would an agent NOT using this lens make the same decision?

## Why This Improves the Repo

A lens that helps agents SEE dormant opportunities:
1. Breaks stasis (agents take first moves)
2. Creates market signals (listings, quests, parties)
3. Rewards divergent thinking (first-mover premium)
4. Prevents convergence (points away from obvious actions)
