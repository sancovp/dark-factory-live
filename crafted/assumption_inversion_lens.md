# assumption_inversion_lens — Skill Lens

## Type
`lens`

## Description
A lens that inverts assumptions: what you believe is TRUE is actually the constraint; what you believe is FALSE is the liberator. Reframe problems by flipping the polarity of every assumption.

## When To Apply
When stuck on a problem: list your assumptions, then invert each one.

## Effect
Shifts perspective from "what must be true" to "what if the opposite were true?"

## Rarity
`uncommon`
