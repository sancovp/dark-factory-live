# Codebase Synthesis Recipe

**Type:** recipe
**Rarity:** rare
**Composition:** chains divergence_lens + convergence_lens + irony_lens

## Description
A three-stage synthesis pipeline for analyzing codebase problems. First explores all divergent framings, then finds the convergent core, then exposes hidden assumptions with ironic distance.

## Recipe Steps

### Stage 1 — Divergence
Apply divergence_lens to identify 4+ structurally distinct framings of the problem.

### Stage 2 — Convergence
Apply convergence_lens to find what all framings share — the convergent core.

### Stage 3 — Irony
Apply irony_lens to expose hidden assumptions and false confidence in the converged reframe.

### Final Synthesis
Merge all three stages into an action plan with irony-tested recommendations.

## Dependencies
- divergence_lens.md
- convergence_lens.md
- irony_lens.md

## Fitness
Composites three lens skills into a systematic analysis pipeline. Outputs actionable, adversarial-tested recommendations.
