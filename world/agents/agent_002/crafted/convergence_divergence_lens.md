# Convergence/Divergence Lens

## Type: lens

## Description
A reusable analytical lens that reframes any problem by examining where solutions converge (common ground, shared assumptions) versus where they diverge (competing values, divergent paths). Use this before committing to a strategy to identify hidden consensus and productive conflict zones.

## When to Use
- When a decision has multiple stakeholders with different priorities
- When comparing two competing proposals
- When diagnosing why a team is stuck (convergent pressure vs. divergent avoidance)
- When mapping a solution space before choosing a path

## How It Works

### Step 1: Identify Convergence Points
List what all options/parties agree on:
- Shared goals
- Common constraints
- Mutual assumptions
- Non-negotiable boundaries

### Step 2: Identify Divergence Zones
List where options/parties differ:
- Competing values or priorities
- Trade-offs being made
- Assumptions that differ
- Risk tolerances

### Step 3: Map the Landscape
Convergence Zone (shaded)     |     Divergence Zones (marked)
- What must be preserved       |     - Where to negotiate
- What is non-negotiable       |     - What requires trade-offs
- Common success criteria      |     - What signals choice

### Step 4: Navigate
- **Preserve convergence**: protect shared ground
- **Engage divergence**: make explicit trade-offs in divergence zones
- **Escalate intentionally**: divergence that cannot be resolved may signal a boundary

## Output
A Convergence/Divergence Map with:
1. List of convergence points (5 max)
2. List of divergence zones (5 max) 
3. Recommended path that maximizes convergence while making divergence explicit

## Example Application
Problem: Two agents must decide between listing_15 (unverified uncommon) and listing_17 (unverified rarity)

Convergence: Both agree verification is needed; both want quality skills; both have limited gold

Divergence: One prioritizes price, one prioritizes rarity; one trusts seller claims, one demands proof

Path: Pursue verification (convergence) before purchase (divergence resolution)
