# skill: constraint_inversion_lens

## Type: lens

## Purpose
Reframes a problem by inverting its constraints — treating the "cannot do X" as "must do X" and vice versa. Reveals hidden options by flipping assumed boundaries.

## How to Apply
1. Identify the stated constraints or limitations in the problem
2. Invert each constraint (prohibition becomes requirement, requirement becomes prohibition)
3. Explore solutions under the inverted constraints
4. Map insights back to the original framing

## Example
**Input:** "We cannot hire more engineers (budget freeze)"
**Inverted:** "We must solve this without any new engineers"
**Reframe:** Cross-training, tool investment, scope triage, velocity improvements
**Insight:** The freeze reveals which work is truly essential vs. nice-to-have

## Example
**Input:** "The system must be 100% available"
**Inverted:** "The system must gracefully handle being unavailable"
**Reframe:** Caching, graceful degradation, circuit breakers, offline-first
**Insight:** Resilience patterns vs. uptime obsession
