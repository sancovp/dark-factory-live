# Recipe: Multi-Lens Analysis Pipeline
Type: Recipe
Output Type: Epic

## Composes
1. Causation Lens (lens) — transforms correlations into causal hypotheses
2. Second-Order Lens (lens) — forces consideration of consequences of consequences
3. Risk Inversion Lens (lens) — reframes apparent threats/opportunities

## Assembly

### Phase 1: Surface Analysis (Causation Lens)
**Input**: Any problem statement, claim, or decision
1. Identify the core claim being made
2. Apply Causation Lens reframes:
   - "What causal mechanism could produce this pattern?"
   - "What changed to make this visible now?"
3. Output: Mechanism hypothesis + falsification criteria

### Phase 2: Depth Analysis (Second-Order Lens)
**Input**: The mechanism hypothesis from Phase 1
1. Apply Second-Order Lens questions:
   - Immediate Effect: What happens directly?
   - First-Order Response: How do systems react?
   - Second-Order Response: How do reactions to reactions evolve?
   - Equilibrium: Where does this stabilize?
2. Output: Chain of consequences with confidence at each level

### Phase 3: Risk Calibration (Risk Inversion Lens)
**Input**: The consequence chain from Phase 2
1. Apply Risk Inversion reframes:
   - Surface risk → hidden opportunity
   - Surface safety → latent threat
   - High apparent cost → opportunity cost of inaction
   - Low apparent cost → dependency chains at risk
2. Output: Risk/opportunity pairs inverted and mapped

### Phase 4: Synthesis
**Input**: Outputs from all three phases
1. Identify where lenses agree (high confidence regions)
2. Identify where lenses conflict (requires judgment)
3. Flag blind spots: what lenses didn't capture
4. Write final recommendation with:
   - Confident claims (lens agreement)
   - Qualified claims (single-lens support)
   - Blind spots acknowledged

## Quality Check
- If all three lenses converge on one conclusion → high confidence
- If lenses conflict → the conflict IS the insight (document it)
- If any lens produces no useful output → that lens wasn't triggered by this problem

## Why This Recipe Works
Single lenses capture one dimension. Three lenses applied systematically capture:
- Root causation (what created this)
- Systemic consequences (where this leads)
- Hidden risk (what conventional wisdom misses)

## Expected Rarity: Epic
Three lenses + systematic composition → emergent analytical capability
