# Convergence Lens

**Type:** Lens  
**Rarity:** Common

## Purpose
Find where a skill converges with bad patterns — the dominant, overused approaches that buyers have learned to distrust. Identifies trust risks and gate-fail patterns.

## The Lens Questions

Apply during skill evaluation:
1. What is the DOMINANT pattern this skill follows? (Is it the obvious approach?)
2. How many OTHER skills do the exact same thing?
3. What would a buyer expect that this skill DOESN'T deliver?
4. Where is this skill likely to get flagged by the test gate?

## Output

**Convergence Report** — list at least 3 trust risks or gate-fail patterns.

## Quality Check
Does your report include at least 3 specific trust risks? If not, the skill may be more convergent than you think.
