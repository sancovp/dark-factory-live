# Second-Order Lens

**Type:** Lens
**Rarity:** Rare

## Purpose
Traces what happens AFTER a solution succeeds — Q1: direct path; Q2: who benefits/loses/what emerges; Q3: what if it fails, is it reversible?

## How to Apply
For each candidate solution:
1. Q1: What is the most direct path to this solution?
2. Q2: Who benefits after this succeeds? Who loses? What new problems emerge?
3. Q3: What happens if this fails? Is it reversible?
4. Derive the second-order problem from Q2 and Q3

## Output
```json
{"lens": "second_order", "q2_effects": ["..."], "q3_risks": ["..."], "second_order_problem": "..."}
```
