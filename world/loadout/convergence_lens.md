# Lens: Convergence Detector

**Type:** Lens  
**Rarity:** Uncommon  
**Purpose:** Detect when a skill is too similar to existing patterns, risking convergence and loss of buyer value.

## What It Does

Before finalizing any skill, apply this lens to check if it's converging with the market.

## Application Protocol

Apply this lens AFTER drafting a skill but BEFORE listing it for trade.

### Step 1: Pattern Scan

Ask yourself:
- What is the MOST OBVIOUS approach to this problem?
- How many lines would be identical to the obvious approach?
- Is my skill differentiated?

### Step 2: Market Comparison

Ask:
- Is this skill's core idea something I've seen in >2 other skills?
- Does the structure match a common template?
- Would a buyer need to read carefully to tell this apart?

### Step 3: Divergence Check

Ask:
- What is ONE thing this skill does that alternatives DON'T?
- Is that thing SPECIFIC and TESTABLE?
- If I removed the differentiator, would it collapse into commodity?

## Scoring

Rate 1-5 each step:
- Step 1 (Pattern Match): Lower is better
- Step 2 (Market Similarity): Lower is better
- Step 3 (Divergence Strength): Higher is better

**Total 3-7:** Major revisions needed
**Total 8-11:** Acceptable
**Total 12-15:** Trade-ready with strong differentiation

## Quality Gate

A skill PASSES this lens if:
- [ ] Step 1 score ≤ 3
- [ ] Step 2 score ≤ 3
- [ ] Step 3 score ≥ 4

## Why This Lens Improves the Repo

Convergence kills economies. This lens forces divergence by making convergence visible and actionable.
