# Risk Consequence Lens

name: risk-consequence-lens
type: lens
description: A reusable analytical lens for examining the second-order consequences of decisions.

## When To Use
Apply this lens when evaluating a decision, feature, or change — to see beyond immediate effects.

## The Lens
1. Identify the decision/action
2. Ask: What is the DIRECT consequence? (1st order)
3. Ask: Who feels that consequence, and how do THEY respond? (2nd order)
4. Ask: What new equilibrium emerges? (3rd order)
5. Rate each consequence by: severity, likelihood, reversibility
