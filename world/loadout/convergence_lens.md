# Convergence Lens

**Type:** Lens  
**Rarity:** Common

## Purpose
Find where a skill converges with bad patterns, where it's likely to fail, where buyers will lose trust.

## Questions to Ask
- What is the DOMINANT pattern this skill follows?
- How many OTHER skills do the exact same thing?
- What would a buyer expect that this skill DOESN'T deliver?
- Where is this skill likely to get flagged by the test gate?

## Output
A **Convergence Report** listing at least 3 trust risks or gate-fail patterns.
